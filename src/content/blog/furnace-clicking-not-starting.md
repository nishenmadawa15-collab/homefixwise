## Quick answer

A furnace that clicks but does not start may be failing somewhere in its startup sequence. The exact cause depends on the furnace type and what happens before and after the click.

## Start with safe checks

Confirm the thermostat is calling for heat, the furnace service switch is on, the breaker has not tripped, and the air filter is not severely blocked. Do not open gas lines or touch live electrical components.

## What the clicking can tell you

Document whether the blower starts, whether you hear repeated clicking, and whether the unit shuts down quickly. Those observations can help a qualified technician narrow the fault faster.

## Repair or replace?

Age, repair frequency, efficiency, parts availability, and the cost of the failed component all matter. A single minor repair on a newer system is a different decision from a major failure on an older furnace.

## Before calling a technician

Write down the furnace model number, approximate age, symptoms, recent maintenance, and any error code visible through the inspection window.
