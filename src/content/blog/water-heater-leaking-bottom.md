## First: protect the area

Keep people away from standing water near electrical equipment. If there is an immediate electrical, gas, or scalding hazard, stop and contact the appropriate professional or utility.

## Common leak locations

Water seen at the bottom may originate higher on the unit, from a valve, connection, or condensation. The visible puddle alone does not identify the failed component.
