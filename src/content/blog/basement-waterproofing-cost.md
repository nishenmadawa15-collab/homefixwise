## The method matters more than a single average

Interior drainage, sump-pump work, crack repair, exterior excavation, grading, and foundation repairs solve different problems and can have very different project scopes.

## Diagnose the water source first

Before comparing prices, identify when water appears, where it enters, and whether drainage or grading problems are visible outside.
