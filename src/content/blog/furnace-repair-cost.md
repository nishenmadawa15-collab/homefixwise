## What determines furnace repair cost?

The biggest variables are the failed part, diagnostic time, labor rate, furnace type, system age, parts availability, and whether the visit is scheduled or emergency service.

## Avoid fake precision

National averages can be useful context, but they should not be presented as a guaranteed quote. A better guide explains the cost drivers and shows readers what information to collect before requesting estimates.
